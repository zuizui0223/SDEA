**No parent numerical result is used as a parameter estimate.** SDEA's 0.65 activation, 0.12 allele frequency, q values and the assurance-cost parameter are constructed choices selected before evaluating their outcomes. SDEA v1 has no biological observations, no experiment with live plants, and no claim of cross-clade replication.

## Evidence levels

- **Mathematical/structural:** counterexamples and negative controls about the *specified one-step kernel*, exactly computed, not fitted.
- **Synthetic/conditional:** directional differences given declared assumptions. Conditions can reverse signs; no numerical ecological universal thresholds.
- **Source-backed but external:** claims reported by each parent repo with their own evidence thresholds, not pooled or reanalysed here.
- **Empirical confirmation:** **none in SDEA v1**. Requires independent prospective measurements and held-out taxa/populations.

**Rule:** do not promote a computed one-step `joint` event to re-evolution, lasting functional recovery, genetic fixation, or established natural lineage. Multiple source repositories discussing the same claim are not independent empirical replicates.

## v2 extension (2026-10-26)

`src/sdea/multigeneration.py` is an **independently declared SDEA state space**, inspired by the above conceptual questions, not a direct numerical replication of the pinned parent sources. In particular, no `anto` route-failure parameter, `egwe` q-allele feedback coefficient or `izu-core` finite-diploid reproductive rate has been imported. The synthetic shared-toolkit collapse after a **visible** expression differs from ANTO v15's failure-triggered common-capacity cost; v2's binomial allele-copy resampling differs from the explicit diploid mating in Model 3. Cross-source *mechanism identity* is not established and must be tested before any quantitative parameter transfer. The exact synthetic frontiers depend on the event/horizon and cannot be added to the source results as independent replications.


## SDEA v3 explicit genetic bridge (2026-10-26)

This **new** one-locus diploid Mendelian reproduction kernel is a constructive robustness challenge to the independent target-allele-copy draw of SDEA v1/v2. [Protocol](PROTOCOL_V3.md), [source](../src/sdea/mendelian.py), [fixed result](../results/v3_mendelian_exact.json). Historical-mating type labels are synthetic proxies for two experimentally held-out genotype arrangements; no ancestry dates or field parentage have been imported. Conditional on successful reproduction, maternal gamete variance and realized viable selfing affect offspring loss risk even after mean target-allele frequency is matched. A selection perturbation reverses the arrangement ranking.
