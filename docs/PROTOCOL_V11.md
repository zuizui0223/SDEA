# SDEA v11 — separate ecological marginal drift from same-mother association drift

**2026-10-28. Post hoc diagnostic after SDEA v10's negative year-held-out result.** This is a retrospective follow-up, not a new preregistered original field experiment, not a third year, not an independent plant radiation, and not a confirmation of SDEA's genetic trait-regain theory. The prior v1–v10 programs and their frozen numerical claims are unchanged.

## Question

Can the observed failure of the same-mother joint good-seed success model to transfer across 2007/2008 be explained by **changes in the success of each route**, **changes in the association between them**, or both? If we restrict the predictive association to a training-year-selected strength, does the held-out-year transfer failure persist?

We apply two **separate** tests:

1. **Descriptive identity (not predictive or causal):** Exactly partition changes in the entire four-state distribution into a vector of changes generated by the year-specific *observed* apical/lateral marginal success rates, plus the vector of changed observed same-mother covariance.
2. **Train-only predictive regularization:** Fit an association-shrinkage weight using leave-one-mother-out log-loss *inside the training year only*. Evaluate two independent proper scores on the entire other year, in both directions. Both predictors retain the **same trained marginal** success rates, so only the strength of the association differs.

Neither test identifies why a maternal seed-route outcome changed. The public file does not simultaneously measure matched pollen identity, pollen deposition, the surviving source of insect damage, effective genotype, descendant reproductive traits, or the same maternal genotypes across years.

The only original biological source is USDA CC0 DOI [10.15482/USDA.ADC/1522649](https://doi.org/10.15482/USDA.ADC/1522649), mirrored by empirical owner [aza3](https://github.com/zuizui0223/aza3) as `data/external/usda_wavyleaf_2007_2008/mother_plants.csv`. The source is read through the **existing `data/v9_source_lock.json` Git commit/blob SHA**; a drift, access failure, or different source schema blocks the run. Source has 106 maternal plant-year records. The one original internally inconsistent record `2007|327` has `ap.hd=0`, `o.hds=1`, but `plt.gsd=20` and is reported separately, leaving 105 coherent original mother-year records (38 in 2007; 67 in 2008). The eight year×treatment categories are `di,dni,ndi,ndni`. We do **not** copy the 106 individual rows into SDEA.

At each same-mother census, `A` means >0 source-defined apical good seeds; `L` means >0 nonapical good seeds. The four mutually exclusive states are `(00,10,01,11)`. These are **contemporaneous anatomical reproductive routes**, not genetic developmental modules and not ancestors and descendants of the same individual.

## An exact additive distributional identity

Let `P_y=(P00,P10,P01,P11)` be the empirical four-state probabilities in one year/treatment stratum, `pAy=P(A=1)`, `pLy=P(L=1)`. The independent-margin distribution is

\[
Q_y=((1-p_{Ay})(1-p_{Ly}),\;p_{Ay}(1-p_{Ly}),\;(1-p_{Ay})p_{Ly},\;p_{Ay}p_{Ly}).
\]

The same-mother binary covariance is `c_y=P11-pAy*pLy`. The residual vector is **exactly** `R_y=P_y-Q_y=(c_y,-c_y,-c_y,c_y)`. For the two observed years, this is a deterministic algebraic equality:

\[
\Delta P = (Q_{2008}-Q_{2007}) + (c_{2008}-c_{2007})(1,-1,-1,1).
\]

We report the full signed four-component vectors. Define `TV(P,Q)=½ Σ|P_i-Q_i|`. Report three **nonadditive** descriptive distances: full observed `TV(P_2007,P_2008)`, product-margin `TV(Q_2007,Q_2008)`, and association-vector norm `2 |c_2008-c_2007|`. The scalar TV distances do **not** sum to a uniquely identified marginal/association **causal** share, because their signed vectors may reinforce/cancel. Check triangle and reverse-triangle bounds; any violation fails the analysis.

**Observed high-drift witness:** `ndni` (no apical damage, ambient herbivory): apical and lateral marginal success both 7/9 (2007) → 2/15 (2008), each −0.644444; covariance −0.0493827→+0.0488889. Thus `TV_joint=0.800000`, `TV_product_margins=0.7017284`, `TV_association_vector=0.1965432`. These last two magnitudes are **not percentages of causal contribution**; they are components of an exact signed vector equality. The source has only 9 and 15 mother-year records in this cell and no independent natural year replication. In `dni` the observed apical route never produced good seeds in either year and the association residual is identically zero; the lateral marginal nevertheless shifts 0.75→0.5833.

**Partial identification from marginals alone:** without measured same-mother joint success, `P(A or L)` only lies in the sharp Fréchet interval `[max(pA,pL), min(1,pA+pL)]`. We report this separately; a broad interval is measurement insufficiency, not observed evidence for an ecological niche interaction.

## Training-only association-shrinkage forecast (v10 stress test)

Reuse SDEA v10's **fixed** categorical alpha=0.5 smoothing: `p_joint_i=(n_i+0.5)/(n+2)`. Derive `p_ind` from that same training law's two marginals, which ensures identical marginal forecasts. Define for a fixed candidate `lambda`:

\[
p_{\lambda}=(1-\lambda)p_{ind}+\lambda p_{joint}.
\]

This is a probability-preserving **only-the-association** intervention for the predictive model. `lambda=0` returns exactly the original independent v10 control; `lambda=1` exactly the original joint v10 model. Intermediate values shrink its covariance without changing either marginal. No arbitrary renormalization or target-year pseudo-count fitting is allowed.

**Candidate grid** (declared for this retrospective diagnostic before running the v11 code): `lambda ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}`. Select **one** lambda shared across the four treatment strata by minimum within-*training-year* leave-one-mother-out natural-log loss. Each mother's category is removed from its training cell before the candidate prediction is generated; it cannot score itself. Break exact ties toward smaller lambda. We do **not** tune lambda using held-out year or categorical Brier. Train on full year after selection, then score **every mother** of the other year, both directions (`2007→2008 n=67`, `2008→2007 n=38`) with two proper scores:

- natural-log four-category loss per held-out mother;
- unhalved categorical Brier error per held-out mother.

Predeclared v11 diagnostic gate (not a biological hypothesis registration): training-selected lambda must beat the independent endpoint **on both proper scores in both held-out directions** to claim an archive-specific replicated improvement. Any sign-mixed result remains `NO_REPLICATED_TRAIN_ONLY_SHRINKAGE_TRANSFER_GAIN`. We additionally show a **held-out lambda grid solely as posthoc sensitivity**, explicitly never use it to select lambda. As v10's heldout-year outcomes were already viewed before this v11 analysis was designed, all v11 hyperparameters and outcomes are post hoc/conditional and must not be promoted to independent prospective validation.

## Executed bounded result

| Train → held-out year | n held out | Lambda selected inside training year | Δ log loss versus independence | Δ categorical Brier versus independence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2007 → 2008 | 67 | **0.25** | **+0.00841011** | **+0.00146613** |
| 2008 → 2007 | 38 | **0.75** | **−0.10375339** | **−0.00124169** |

Thus the mild association-shrinkage policy *still fails* in the forward 2007→2008 direction, even though the reverse direction improves under training-only tuning. No single robust cross-year gain is established. For comparison, the old `lambda=1` SDEA v10 outcome was positive log loss difference in 2007→2008 and mixed proper-score signs in 2008→2007; v10's negative primary result remains valid and unchanged.

## Negative controls and rejection gates

1. Exact v10 independent and joint endpoints must match `lambda=0/1` without arithmetic discrepancy; all `lambda` values conserve probabilities and **both** training marginals exactly.
2. Malformed source records, changed original source blob, missing group or mother, invalid/duplicate candidate lambda, missing year, nonfinite score or probability fail closed; never infer a natural ecological zero from an unavailable source.
3. The full signed-vector decomposition must close for all four treatment strata; empirical any-route must lie inside sharp Fréchet bounds. TV *magnitudes* are not interpreted as a uniquely additive causal breakdown.
4. Altering only the *test-year* four-state distribution must **not alter the training-year-selected lambda or any model prediction** from training cells; it may change held-out scores.
5. The train-only chosen lambda must not be selected after viewing a preferred test-year metric; lambda grid scores on held-out data are supplementary diagnostics only.
6. Refit original v10 `lambda=1` held-out per-mother log and Brier differences exactly to machine precision; this stage must not rewrite its negative result.
7. No `Brier/significance` confidence intervals from these 105 archived mothers are population-level temporal uncertainty intervals; two years are not an exchangeable sample of ecological regimes.
8. No phylogenetically identified ancestral flower allele, same-individual colour-pigment regulatory assay, heritable descendent expression, or seedling recruitment exists in this source. Preserve `MECHANISM_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` rather than inferring evolutionary trait regain.
